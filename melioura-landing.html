This is a mineral, not a sleeping pill, so it does not work instantly. Most people take 2 to 4 weeks of daily use to notice a change — which is why the 3-bottle package is better value.

Will it loosen my bowels like other magnesium?

That is exactly why we use glycinate instead of oxide — it is the gentlest form on the stomach. If you are sensitive, start with a smaller amount and see how you feel.

Please talk to your doctor first if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, have a kidney or heart condition, or are taking any medication.
